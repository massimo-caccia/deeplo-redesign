Science Diplomacy, Multilateralism and Global Challenges

22–27 June 2026 · Geneva, Switzerland
An off-campus course of the Université de Montréal (INT6240K), organised by CÉRIUM as part of the SDG Summer School Geneva 2026.

Over a week of lectures, workshops and debates, the school explored what science diplomacy is and how it is practised, including the role of data science. Participants:

  • clarified the concepts and practices of science diplomacy;
  • mapped its actors, from the scientific and academic communities to the diplomatic ones;
  • studied the role of multilateral bodies such as the ITU, WHO and WMO;
  • examined local, national and international stakes, and the link with the Sustainable Development Goals;
  • built skills in diplomatic strategy and in research and innovation strategy.

The school also asked whether there is a distinctly francophone approach to science diplomacy.

Course director: Michel Audet (HEC Montréal), an academic partner of the Chair.
Partner universities: Université Cheikh Anta Diop de Dakar, Université libre de Bruxelles, Université de Montréal, Université de Genève.
