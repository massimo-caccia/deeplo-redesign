A Canadian Strategy to Catalyse the Power of Science Diplomacy

Summit · 15–18 June 2026 · Université de Montréal, MIL campus

OverviewSummer schoolSeminarsSummitsPublications

From 15 to 18 June 2026, the Université de Montréal, McGill University and the University of Calgary brought more than 300 diplomats, researchers, policymakers, industry leaders and civil society representatives to the MIL campus, with support from the Fonds de recherche du Québec, the Swiss Confederation and GESDA (Geneva Science and Diplomacy Anticipator).

Over four days of panels, lectures and training sessions, the summit set out to strengthen Canada’s role in science diplomacy and to lay the groundwork for a pan-Canadian network linking research, diplomacy, innovation and public policy. Topics ranged from artificial intelligence and quantum technologies to the Arctic, water, climate and biodiversity, open science, and public trust in science.

The Chair at the summit

  • Catherine Régis sat on the steering and scientific committees. She spoke on the panel Artificial Intelligence, Technology Governance and Diplomacy (16 June) and gave closing remarks (18 June).
  • Michel Audet, a member of the steering and scientific committees, moderated the same AI panel.
  • Yoshua Bengio gave the public opening lecture, Science Diplomacy Without Borders, with astronaut David Saint-Jacques (15 June).
  • Mario Rivero-Huguet, a member of the steering and scientific committees, moderated the panel Science Diplomacy in a Changing Arctic (17 June).
  • Gaëlle Foucault spoke on the panel Next-Generation Perspectives on Science Diplomacy (17 June).
