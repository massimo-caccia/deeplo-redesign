Juan David Gutiérrez

Associate Professor, School of Government, Universidad de los Andes; Director, Master's Program in Law, Technology, and Digital Society
Universidad de los Andes · Colombia
🌐 https://algoritmos.uniandes.edu.co/

Biography

Juan David Gutiérrez is an associate professor at the School of Government at Universidad de los Andes in Colombia, where he teaches and conducts research on public policy, regulation, and artificial intelligence. He is the director of the Master’s program in Law, Technology, and Digital Society at the Law School of Universidad de los Andes. He holds a PhD and an MSc in Public Policy from the University of Oxford, an LLM from Bologna and Erasmus universities, and a law degree from Javeriana University. He leads the Systems of Public Algorithms project at Universidad de los Andes and serves as an expert for Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence (GPAI), co-leading the Algorithmic Transparency in the Public Sector project, and is a member of UNESCO’s Network of Experts on AI and Rule of Law. He also consults on AI governance for UNESCO, UNDP, ECLAC, and ITU, as well as for international civil society organizations.
