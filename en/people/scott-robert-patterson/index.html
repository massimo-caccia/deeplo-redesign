Scott Robert Patterson

Postdoctoral Researcher
Université de Montréal · Canada
🌐 https://patterson-s.github.io/

Biography

Scott Robert Patterson is a postdoctoral researcher at the Mila Quebec AI Institute (MILA) and at the Centre de Recherche en Droit Public (CRDP) of the Université de Montréal. His research focuses on global AI governance, with a particular emphasis on the emerging landscape of norms, the politics of open source AI development, and the governance role of generative AI companies.

Scott is also an expert in research methods for computational social science, especially regarding natural language processing (NLP). He has published research in peer reviewed journals, including International Studies Review, the Journal of Quantitative Description, Global Studies Quarterly and the Journal of International Relations and Development. Prior to his current role, Scott was a postdoctoral researcher in global AI governance in the Department of Political Science at the Université de Montréal.

Scott completed his Ph.D. in Political Science at McGill University in 2025. His dissertation, entitled “Computation and Interpretation in International Relations,” focused on how NLP methods can be used with an interpretive epistemology to make sense of diplomatic language. He also holds a Master’s in Advanced International Studies from the Diplomatic Academy of Vienna. In addition to his academic work, Scott has also consulted on issues related to organizational adoption of AI, including for the Green Climate Fund, the International Development Research Centre, and McGill University. He speaks German, French, and Spanish.
