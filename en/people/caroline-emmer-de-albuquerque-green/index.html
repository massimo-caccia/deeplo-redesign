Caroline Emmer De Albuquerque Green

Director of Research and Head of Public Engagement, Institute for Ethics in AI, University of Oxford
University of Oxford · United Kingdom

Biography

Caroline Green is Director of Research and Head of Public Engagement at the Institute for Ethics in AI at the University of Oxford. Her research focuses on AI and human rights, specifically in the fields of health and social care. As Director of Research, she also leads the Accelerator Fellowship Programme at the Institute. She holds an LLB (Hons) from the University of Edinburgh, an MSc in Human Rights from the LSE, an MA in Investigative Journalism from City University, and a PhD in Gerontology from King’s College London.
