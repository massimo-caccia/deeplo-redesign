Mario Rivero-Huguet

Advisor
British High Commission to Canada · Canada

Biography

Dr Mario Rivero-Huguet is a science diplomacy and emerging technologies expert with more than 25 years of experience at the intersection of science, technology, innovation, public policy and international relations. For over 14 years he has served as Head of Science and Technology at the British High Commission to Canada based in Montréal and was the National AI Lead, where he led initiatives on AI governance, safety and ethical AI, advanced computing infrastructure and international research collaboration. Earlier in his career, he worked at Canada’s International Development Research Centre and the Commission for Environmental Cooperation in North America, and taught at Canadian and international universities. He is also a member of the OECD Network of Experts on Artificial Intelligence and an active contributor to global science diplomacy and science advice initiatives. He holds a PhD in Environmental Health from McGill University.
