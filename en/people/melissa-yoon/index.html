Melissa Yoon

Professor, School of Law and Department of Artificial Intelligence, Hanyang University
Hanyang University · South Korea

Biography

Melissa Yoon is a Professor at the Hanyang University School of Law and the Department of Artificial Intelligence in Seoul, South Korea, specializing in administrative law and regulatory governance. She holds a B.Sc. in Biochemistry and an M.Sc. in Physiology from McGill University, a B.C.L. & LL.B. in civil and common law from McGill (2005), and a PhD in Administrative Law from Seoul National University (2009). She is licensed to practice as an Attorney at Law in New York (USA) and Ontario (Canada), and previously worked at Smart & Biggar/Fetherstonhaugh in Canada. She has served on several South Korean government advisory bodies, including the Legal Interpretation Review Committee of the Ministry of Government Legislation (2014), and is an active member of the Korean Public Law Association and the Administrative Law Association. She co-authored the Global Policy Brief on AI and Government (2026), titled Governing with AI: Four Actions to Build a Transformative and Resilient Public Administration, produced jointly by IVADO and the AI + Society Initiative at the University of Ottawa.
