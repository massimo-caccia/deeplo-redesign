Rashid Mushkani

Postdoctoral Researcher
Université de Montréal · Canada

Biography

Rashid Mushkani works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, urban governance, and participatory design, with a focus on AI pluralism. He explores how AI can reflect diverse values and perspectives, and how communities can meaningfully shape its development and use. His doctoral research at Université de Montréal advances an “urban right to AI,” connecting democratic participation, spatial justice, and inclusive public spaces. Through community-engaged research and governance design, he develops approaches to making AI more accountable to the people and places it serves.
