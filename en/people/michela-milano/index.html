Michela Milano

Full Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Università di Bologna
University of Bologna · Italy

Biography

Michela Milano is Full Professor of Artificial Intelligence at DISI – Università di Bologna. She is past Deputy President of the European Association of Artificial Intelligence (EurAI), past Executive Councilor of the Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI), and past member of the Executive Committees of the Association for Constraint Programming and of the Italian Association of Artificial Intelligence.

Her research focuses on AI with particular emphasis on decision support and optimization systems, spanning both theoretical and practical aspects in fields including energy, mobility, computing, policy-making, and sustainability. She is Editor-in-Chief of the Constraints Journal, a member of the Editorial Board of ACM Computing Surveys for AI, and past Area Editor of the INFORMS Journal on Computing in Logic, Constraint, and Optimization. She has edited two collections on hybrid optimization and authored more than 170 papers in peer-reviewed international conferences and journals. She has coordinated numerous European, Italian, and regional projects and leads collaborations with industry. In 2016, she received the Google Faculty Research Award for her work on deep networks in combinatorial optimization.
