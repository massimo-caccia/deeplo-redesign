Jean-François Gagné

Lecturer, Department of Political Science, Université de Montréal; Researcher, Centre d'études et de recherches internationales, Université de Montréal
Université de Montréal · Canada

Biography

Jean-François Gagné holds a doctorate in political science from the Université de Montréal (2013). His research focuses on the comparative analysis of national AI strategies and the impacts of technology on democracy. He is responsible for the algorithmic governance working group of the Déclaration de Montréal IA Responsable. He is currently an associate member of the Centre d’études d’Asie de l’Est (CÉTASE), a lecturer, and an academic programs advisor at the Université de Montréal. He has previously served as a researcher at the Chaire Raoul-Dandurand en études stratégiques et diplomatiques and as a policy analyst at Export Development Canada.
