Gaëlle Foucault

Postdoctoral researcher and lecturer in international law
Université de Montréal · Canada
🌐 https://www.linkedin.com/in/gaelle-foucault-356a35142/

Biography

Gaëlle Foucault is a postdoctoral researcher and lecturer at the Faculty of Law of the Université de Montréal. She is a recipient of IVADO’s postdoctoral excellence funding program, an interdisciplinary and cross-sectoral consortium dedicated to research, training, and knowledge mobilization. Her research focuses on the role of international law and international organizations in the governance of global challenges, such as artificial intelligence. Gaëlle Foucault is affiliated with MILA, the CRDP, IVADO, and H-Pod. She coordinates the “Science Diplomacy and Global AI Governance” Chair and the H-Pod research centre. She holds a doctorate in international law from UdeM, a master’s degree in international law from Université Jean-Moulin, Lyon III (France), and a bachelor’s degree in law from Université Panthéon-Assas, Paris II (France).
