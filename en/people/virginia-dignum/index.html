Virginia Dignum

Professor in Responsible Artificial Intelligence; Director, AI Policy Lab, Umeå universitet
Umeå University · Sweden

Biography

Virginia Dignum is Professor in Responsible Artificial Intelligence and Director of the AI Policy Lab at Umeå universitet. Her research focuses on the complex interconnections and interdependencies between people, organizations, and technology, ranging from the engineering of practical applications and simulations to the development of formal theories that integrate agency and organization. Her current research directions include responsible AI — exploring the moral and ethical issues arising from autonomous intelligent agents and how their activity can be regulated; the analysis and formalization of social interaction, studying the effects of social and organizational structure through formal theories and a computational architecture for agent deliberation based on social practices; and the design and evaluation of human-agent teamwork, examining how people negotiate, trust, and cooperate with autonomous cognitive entities in social settings.

Active in international AI policy, she is a member of the Global Partnership on AI (GPAI), the Dutch AI Alliance (ALLAI-NL), the World Economic Forum Council on AI, and the High-Level Expert Group on the Implementation of the UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation. She has previously served on the European Commission High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence, the IEEE Global Initiative on Ethically Aligned Design of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems, and several other bodies. She is also associated with the Faculty of Technology, Policy and Management at the Delft University of Technology.

In recognition of her research, she was elected to the Swedish Royal Academy of Engineering Sciences (IVA) in 2020 and appointed Fellow of the European Artificial Intelligence Association (EURAI) in 2018.
