François Grey

Vice Dean for Pedagogical Innovation; Associate Professor, Research Institute for Statistics and Information Science, Université de Genève
Université de Genève · Switzerland

Biography

François Grey is a physicist by training with a background in nanotechnology and over 20 years of practical experience in citizen science, as well as broad experience in innovation management gained at universities, national laboratories, and international science facilities. In 2009, he established a citizen science partnership between the Université de Genève, CERN, and the UN Institute for Training and Research (UNITAR) known as Citizen Cyberlab. As Associate Professor at the Centre Universitaire d’Informatique (CUI) of the Université de Genève since 2014, he coordinates Citizen Cyberlab, overseeing the development of crowdsourced research technologies, exploring the broader societal impact of citizen science, and promoting public participation in science through hackathons. He also teaches courses on Open Science and Crowdsourcing & AI at the bachelor and master levels. Since 2016, he has directed the Geneva Tsinghua Initiative, a comprehensive education programme for the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and since 2022 he has held an additional appointment as associate professor at the Geneva School of Economics and Management.
