Biography

Georgyan Dayron is a law student at Université de Montréal. He holds a Bachelor of Civil Law and a University Diploma in Common Law from Université Paris-Panthéon-Assas (France), as well as a master’s degree in comparative Common Law from Université de Montréal. His research paper treats of intellectual property.
