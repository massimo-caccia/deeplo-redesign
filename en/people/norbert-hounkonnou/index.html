Norbert Hounkonnou

Professor of Mathematics and Physics, University of Abomey-Calavi
Université d'Abomey-Calavi · Benin

Biography

Mahouton Norbert Hounkonnou is a professor of mathematics and physics at the University of Abomey-Calavi. He is president of the Network of African Academies of Sciences, former president of the National Academy of Sciences, Arts and Letters of Benin, and co-president of the Network of African, European and Mediterranean Academies for Science Education. He was a member of the UNESCO Scientific Council for the International Basic Sciences Programme and of the InterAcademy Partnership (IAP) working group on harnessing science, engineering and medicine to address Africa’s challenges.
