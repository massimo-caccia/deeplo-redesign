Catherine Régis

Director of Social Innovation and International Policy
Université de Montréal · Canada

Biography

Catherine Régis is a Full Professor of Law at Université de Montréal where she also holds an International Research Chair on Science Diplomacy and Global Governance of Artificial Intelligence (AI). She concurrently holds an Associate Academic appointment at Mila – Québec AI Institute, alongside a Canada CIFAR AI Chair. She is also Co-director of the Canadian AI Safety Institute Research Program at CIFAR and Director of Social Innovation and International Policy at IVADO, Canada’s leading research, training, and knowledge mobilization consortium in AI. Her research affiliations include a role as Senior Research Associate at the Intellectual Forum, Jesus College, University of Cambridge, as well as memberships in the Public Law Research Center (CRDP), the Montreal Centre for International Studies (CERIUM), and the Maison des affaires publiques et internationales at Université de Montréal.

She is active on the international scene. In 2022, she was appointed cochair, for a two-year term, of the Working Group on Responsible AI of the Global Partnership on AI (GPAI), which comprises more than 30 member states, and, from 2020 to 2024, she led the Working Group on Responsible Digital Innovation and AI of the U7+ Alliance, which includes more than 50 universities from around the world. That same year, in 2022, she was selected as a Fellow of the UN’s Institute for Training and Research’s (UNITAR) science diplomacy program, and, since 2024, she has served on the Technical Committee for UNESCO’s AI and the Rule of Law Program. In 2025, she was a member of the G7 Gender Equality Advisory Council under the Government of Canada.

Her research interests include AI governance and security at the national and international levels, human rights in the age of AI, the protection of democratic institutions, and responsible digital innovation in healthcare. Her recent publications include: Régis, C., Denis, J.-L., Axente, M. L. & Kishimoto, A. (eds). Human-Centered AI: A Multidisciplinary Perspective for Policy-Makers, Auditors and Users, Routledge & CRC Press, 2024; Emmer De Albuquerque Green, C. & Régis, C. (Eds). Patients, Power and AI, Wiley, in press, 2026; Régis, C., Foucault, G. & Audet, M. (2026). Un groupe d’experts pour éclairer la gouvernance de l’IA par la science, The Conversation, May 13

She has presented her work at institutions such as the OECD, the World Health Organization (WHO), the United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research (UNIDIR), the Alan Turing Institute, the Supreme Court of Canada, The Wall Street Journal, and the Aspen Ministers Forum; at more than 20 universities worldwide (e.g., Costa Rica, Edinburgh, Georgetown, Osaka, Oxford, Sciences Po Paris, Sorbonne, Toronto); and at high-level conferences, including the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park in 2023 and NeurIPS. She is also involved in the development of science diplomacy, which aims to build leadership and communication skills to bridge science and policymaking at the national and international levels.

Among her recent honors, in 2026, she was appointed an Officer of the Order of Montréal in recognition of her outstanding contribution to the development of responsible AI, and in 2025, she was named one of the Inspiring Women in AI by Taylor & Francis’ UK-based blog.
