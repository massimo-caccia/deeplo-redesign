Michel Audet

Associate Professor, HEC Montréal
HEC Montréal · Canada

Biography

Michel Audet holds a bachelor’s and master’s degree in industrial relations from Université Laval in Quebec City, and earned a doctorate in industrial relations from the Université de Montréal in 1992. He also studied at the Institute of Industrial Relations at the University of California, Berkeley.

For several years, he was a visiting professor at the École Supérieure de Commerce de Paris. He also served as Director of Innovation and Transfer and Scientific Director at the Centre francophone d’informatisation des organisations (CEFRIO), and as a full professor in the Department of Industrial Relations at Université Laval, where he directed the Institut Technologies de l’information et Sociétés.

Following his academic career, he moved into diplomacy and public administration, successively serving as Quebec’s Representative to the Permanent Delegation of Canada to UNESCO, Commissioner General of the World Forum of the French Language for the OIF, Deputy Minister at the Ministry of International Relations, Francophonie and Foreign Trade, Quebec’s Delegate General in Brussels and at the European Union, Sherpa to the Premier for the Francophonie, and Special Envoy for Africa. As Deputy Minister, he led the network of 28 Quebec diplomatic posts in 17 countries across four continents. He played an active role in the implementation of two major international instruments for Quebec: the UNESCO Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions, and the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) between Canada and the European Union.
