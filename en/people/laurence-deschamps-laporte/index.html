Laurence Deschamps-Laporte

Director of Research Centre d'études et de recherches internationales; Assistant Professor, Department of Political Science, Université de Montréal
CÉRIUM, Université de Montréal · Canada
🌐 https://cerium.umontreal.ca/accueil/

Biography

Laurence Deschamps-Laporte is Director of Research at CÉRIUM (Centre d’études et de recherches internationales de l’Université de Montréal), where she is also an Assistant Professor of Political Science. Her research focuses on foreign policy, feminism, international relations, and Middle East politics. In 2024, she was a visiting scholar at Exeter College and the Department of Politics and International Relations at the University of Oxford. Laurence Deschamps-Laporte has advised three Canadian Ministers of Foreign Affairs, including as Chief of Staff. In 2023, she was named one of the 50 most influential people in Canadian foreign policy by the Hill Times. She previously provided strategic advice to global philanthropic organizations and public sector organizations as a consultant. She holds a DPhil and an MPhil from the University of Oxford, where she was a Rhodes Scholar, and a BA from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she was a Morehead-Cain Scholar.
