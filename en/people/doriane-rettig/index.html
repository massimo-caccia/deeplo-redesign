Doriane Rettig

Postdoctoral Researcher
Université de Montréal · Canada

Biography

Doriane Rettig is a postdoctoral researcher at the Université de Montréal. Her research focuses on assessing the impact of artificial intelligence on human rights. Her doctoral thesis, defended at Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne (France), examined personal autonomy in light of digital practices. Her work thus lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence law, personal data and digital services. She also holds a Master 2 in digital law from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, as well as a Master 1 and a bachelor’s degree in French and English law awarded jointly by Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and King’s College London (United Kingdom).
